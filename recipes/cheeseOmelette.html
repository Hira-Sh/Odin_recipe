Cheese Omelette Recipes

Description

Cheese omelette is simple,classic, yet divine!!! Enjoy while hot and and melty!!

Ingriedients:

  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon water
  • 1 tablespoon shredded cheese such as cheddar or mozzarella
  • 1 teaspoon unsalted butter

How To Make:

  1. In a bowl, beat the egg with water until well mixed.

  2. Heat butter until melted, then coat the bottom of the pan with the melted butter.

  3. Pour the egg mixture into the pan, tilting to spread evenly. Cook without stirring until the edges begin to set.

  4. Gently push cooked edges toward the center, tilting the pan to allow uncooked egg to flow into the empty space.

  5. When the egg is set and the bottom is light golden, sprinkle cheese over one half of the egg.

  6. Fold the omelet in half with a spatula and cook for another 30 seconds to melt the cheese.

  7. Slide the omelet onto a plate and serve immediately.
